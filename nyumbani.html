💡 What drives price differences?

Security: Gated or fenced areas like Kansenshi command premiums due to electric fencing and security guards.
Proximity: Areas close to town centres, shopping malls, or schools tend to be pricier.
Infrastructure: Reliable ZESCO power and borehole water vs. municipal connections affect rent.
Road quality: Well-tarred areas attract higher rents than gravel-road neighbourhoods.

🔐 About Seller Verification

All marketplace sellers must submit a valid NRC (National Registration Card) and business details before listing. Verified sellers are confirmed as real individuals or registered businesses. Unverified seller listings are shown with a warning — contact them at your own risk. NyumbaNi is not liable for unverified transactions.

How NyumbaNi Works

The platform built for free, direct connections between owners and tenants in Zambia.

1

Landlords post directly

Property owners post their own listings with real photos and accurate descriptions. No middlemen reposting stolen photos from Facebook.

2

Tenants browse for free

Anyone can search, filter, and view property details completely free — no account needed. Compare prices by neighbourhood using Price Compare.

3

Contact the owner directly

When you find a property you like, view the owner's contact details right on the listing — completely free, no unlocking, no waiting.

4

Connect and view the property

Call or WhatsApp the landlord directly to arrange a viewing. No fees. No commission. Ever.
